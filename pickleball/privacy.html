Paddlecall Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026 · Whizbang Apps

Paddlecall is a score keeper for pickleball on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, made by Whizbang Apps. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

  • Your matches, players and stats stay on your devices and in your own iCloud account. We can't see them.
  • The app has no ads and doesn't track you across other apps or websites.
  • With your permission, the app sends a small, fixed set of anonymous usage events to Mixpanel so we can see which features are used.
  • If you buy Paddlecall Pro, RevenueCat handles the purchase record.

Information that stays with you

  • Matches, scores, players and names are stored on your device and synced through your private iCloud database by Apple's CloudKit. We have no access to it.
  • Health data. On Apple Watch, Paddlecall can record a pickleball workout in Apple Health and read its heart rate and active energy. The watch passes the match's average and peak heart rate, calories and duration to the iPhone app to show on the match summary. They are kept on your devices only and are not uploaded to iCloud. Health data stays under your control and we never receive it. You can change access in the Health app at any time.
  • Match links. When you share a match by link or QR code, the match details are in the part of the link after the #, which your browser doesn't send to our website.

Anonymous usage analytics (Mixpanel)

We use Mixpanel, hosted in the EU, to count how features are used: for example that a match was started or completed, or that a share card was created. Events never include player names, typed text or scores. Mixpanel's automatic data collection and IP-based location are turned off.

Events are tied to a random identifier created by the app, not to your name, email, Apple ID or advertising identifier. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, analytics are off until you agree. Elsewhere they are on by default. You can turn them off at any time in Settings, under Privacy.

Purchases (RevenueCat)

If you buy Paddlecall Pro, the purchase is made through Apple. RevenueCat receives the purchase record from Apple so the app can unlock Pro on your devices. It uses an anonymous app user ID, and the app has no accounts. The anonymous analytics identifier is shared with RevenueCat so purchase events can be counted.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier.
  • No selling or sharing of data with data brokers.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

Paddlecall isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices

  • Turn analytics off in Settings, under Privacy.
  • Remove Health access in the Health app.
  • Delete matches in the app. They are removed from iCloud too. Deleting the app removes its local data.
