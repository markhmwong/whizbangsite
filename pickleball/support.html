PickleBro Support

Whizbang Apps

PickleBro keeps score for pickleball on Apple Watch, iPhone and iPad. If something isn't working, or you have an idea, we'd love to hear from you.

Contact us

Email hello@whizbangapps.dev. The quickest way is Contact Me in PickleBro's Settings, which adds your device and app version for us.

Common questions

Does PickleBro need my iPhone on court?

No. You can start and score a match on Apple Watch alone. The match moves to your iPhone the next time they're together.

The server or side looks wrong. What do I do?

Tap undo on the watch or phone to step back one rally, then score it again. You can undo or redo any point, and edit a finished match from its summary on iPhone.

My matches aren't showing on my other devices.

Check that you're signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud on, and that iCloud Sync is on in PickleBro's Settings. If iCloud storage is full, matches stay on your device until you free up space.

How do I turn the voice umpire on or off?

On iPhone, open Settings in PickleBro and use the Voice umpire switch. It speaks through your phone's speaker, AirPods or a connected speaker.

I bought PickleBro Pro but it's locked.

Open Settings in PickleBro and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. Pro unlocks on every device with that account.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose PickleBro. A lifetime purchase never renews, so there's nothing to cancel.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I stop sharing usage data?

Yes. Turn off Share usage data in PickleBro's Settings, under Privacy. See the privacy policy for what's collected.
